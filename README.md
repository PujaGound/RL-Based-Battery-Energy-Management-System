Reinforcement Learning-Based Battery Energy Management System for Electric Vehicles
Project Overview
This project presents a Reinforcement Learning (DDPG) based Battery Energy Management System (BEMS) for Electric Vehicles using MATLAB/Simulink. The objective is to improve battery performance, energy efficiency, and State of Charge (SoC) management under varying load conditions.
Objectives
Develop an intelligent Battery Energy Management System.
Compare conventional control with Reinforcement Learning.
Improve battery efficiency and SoC stability.
Reduce voltage variation and current stress.
Evaluate controller performance under different driving conditions.

Results
The RL-based controller demonstrated improved energy management by maintaining battery operating conditions while reducing voltage fluctuations and current stress compared with the baseline controller.
